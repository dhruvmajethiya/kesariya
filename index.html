Our Roots & Heritage

About Kesariya Catering

A heritage of royal hospitality, uncompromised purity, and enchanting celebration styling in Surat.

Curating Royal Feasts with Shuddh Shakahari Values

Founded by Bharatbhai Gajera, Kesariya Catering was born from a deep reverence for the auspicious saffron (Kesar) and the sacred tradition of Indian hospitality (Atithi Devo Bhava). Based in Umragam, Surat, we have established a new benchmark for outdoor catering and event production.

Unlike ordinary caterers who treat food and decoration as separate chores, Kesariya synchronizes 100% Pure Vegetarian culinary excellence with bespoke venue styling, ensuring hosts experience effortless luxury and guests cherish lifelong memories.
